# RN Affirmations — "For the Nurse" Cards

**Source: Ting Lott's own book, *Refreshed for the Calling: 365 Days of
Inspiration, Poems, and Scripture for Nurses*** (T. Lott Creative / ARTISY).
Per Ting's direction (Nov 4, 2026: "Use it with RN Command"), these twelve
cards are drawn FROM the book — its daily reflection and poem lines, lightly
adapted into short affirmation-card form where a line needed trimming to fit
a card. Wording stays faithful to his text; each card lists the day it came
from. Delivery PDF: ~/workspace/365-nursing-poems/real/delivery/Refreshed-for-the-Calling-Ting-Lott.pdf

They ship in the Copilot Edition build as the "For the Nurse" card in the
Copilot tab — credited on the card as drawn from *Refreshed for the Calling*.

1. **Established Hands (Day 2)** — Your hands start IVs, turn patients, hold
   fingers, and chart late into the night. Offer them to God today — and ask
   Him to establish what they do.
2. **Heartily, as to the Lord (Day 3)** — Some tasks no one will ever notice:
   the extra blanket, the call light answered quickly, the gentle word at
   3 a.m. God notices every one.
3. **The Greatest Is Love (Day 34)** — The charts will be filed, the shifts
   will fade. After the charting is done, love is what remains.
4. **Perfect Peace (Day 29)** — Peace is not the absence of alarms. It is a
   mind anchored in God while the alarms are sounding.
5. **A Soft Answer (Day 39)** — Frightened and hurting people sometimes lash
   out. A calm, gentle reply is a nurse's quiet superpower.
6. **Endure Hardness (Day 69)** — Enduring is not the same as suffering in
   silence. A good soldier also rests, eats, and relies on the team.
7. **Work Not Forgotten (Day 12)** — Unthanked, unseen, the work goes on —
   but nothing slips past Heaven's eyes.
8. **Carrying Together (Day 11)** — Nurses carry each other as well as
   patients. Notice a coworker who is struggling, and lighten their load in
   some small way.
9. **Visiting the Afflicted (Day 50)** — Being present with the lonely and
   grieving is holy work. Do not underestimate a few minutes of your
   attention.
10. **As Thy Days (Day 65)** — My shoes may wear, my feet may ache, and
    miles pile up each day. But as my days, so is my strength — He promised
    it that way.
11. **A Season for Every Purpose (Day 15)** — There is a time to labor hard,
    a time to rest and mend.
12. **A New Thing (Day 1)** — Fresh page, fresh start, a quiet, hopeful
    spark. He makes a way where none is seen.

*Adaptation notes: cards 2, 4, 5, 6, 9 are the book's reflection lines
essentially verbatim. Cards 7, 10, 11, 12 are the book's poem lines with
line breaks folded into sentences. Cards 1, 3, 8 join or trim the book's own
sentences; no words were added that change meaning, and no outside text was
used.*
